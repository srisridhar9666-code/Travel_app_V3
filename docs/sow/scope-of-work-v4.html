This document represents the complete, fresh, and unified Scope of Work for Sriyatra, the enterprise field logistics, travel fulfillment, vehicle operations, and employee expense reimbursement platform for DesignBoxed Innovations Pvt. Ltd. It provides a comprehensive, end-to-end specification of all system capabilities, workflows, user roles, policies, and technical delivery requirements.

Platform Architecture Overview: (1) Six discrete role portals, (2) Universal mandatory two-tier approval (Manager → Admin → Vendor), (3) Full Campaign and Team management by Managers, (4) Hotel occupancy evaluation (Single vs. Double default), (5) Vendor hotel sourcing (2–5 options with locations) & employee selection, (6) Exact same-gender co-stay matching, (7) Manual hotel check-in/out verification, (8) Daily cab hire telemetry with start/end meter photos and auto-timestamps (Total Distance = End KM − Start KM), (9) Comprehensive employee expense reimbursement module (Food flat ₹400/day for all cities) audited and settled with UTR by the Finance Team, (10) Tamper-evident cryptographic audit logging.

1. User Hierarchy & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

The platform establishes six distinct, ranked user tiers, each with dedicated login credentials, scoped views, operational queues, and permission boundaries:

Role User Type Key Responsibilities & Permissions
1. Users (Ground Staff) Field personnel & team members (~100 staff) Raise requests for Flights, Trains, Buses, Cabs, and Hotels; choose hotel from vendor proposals; select room-sharing with same-gender colleagues; perform manual hotel check-in/out; upload cab start/end odometer photos; submit reimbursement claims with bills (Food flat ₹400/day, transit/auto, tolls/parking).
2. Managers Field Managers & Team Leads Mandatory Level-1 Approver for all team requests; Full Campaign Control (create, edit, pause, delete campaigns); Full Team Control (add, edit, delete team members); evaluate Single vs. Double hotel occupancy; review and endorse team reimbursement claims; inspect team rosters and itineraries.
3. Vendors Contracted travel agents, hotel partners, cab fleets Dedicated portal; receives approved bookings; proposes 2–5 hotel options with addresses/amenities for hotel requests; assigns vehicle/driver for cabs; uploads booking vouchers and e-tickets; submits vendor invoices for completed trips.
4. Finance Team Accounts & Finance Officers Dedicated audit queue for employee reimbursement claims; enforces policy rate caps (Food: flat ₹400/day for all cities; unbilled auto max ₹150/day); approves/partially approves/rejects claims; issues disbursements and records bank UTR/transaction references; cross-audits cab meter KM against vendor bills.
5. Admin Operations & Travel Desk (~10 staff) Mandatory Level-2 Approver; confirms occupancy decisions; dispatches approved requests to vendors; manages departments, system settings, and city tier policies; monitors live operations and cost analytics; direct booking fallback via Gemini AI.
6. Super Admin Executive Leadership System governance; final sign-off on consolidated vendor invoices; user role administration; configuration of policy limits and city categories; tamper-evident audit ledger inspection and data purge schedules.

1.1 Sign-In, Accounts & Security

  • No Self-Registration: All user accounts are provisioned by an administrator. Single-use invitation link (valid 72 hours) dispatched via email or shared over WhatsApp/SMS.
  • Password Policy: Minimum 10 characters. Self-service reset link valid for 2 hours. 5 consecutive incorrect attempts lock the account for 15 minutes. Sessions last 8 hours.
  • Tab Isolation: Browser tabs maintain independent sessions, allowing administrators to test employee-facing workflows concurrently.
  • Theme & Devices: Native Light, Dark, or System theme persisted per user across all devices. Mobile-first responsive layout with full desktop view option on mobile devices.

2. Campaign & Project Management

  • Full Campaign Lifecycle Control: Managers and Admins have complete control to create, edit, pause, complete, and delete campaigns. Campaigns with historical requests can be archived (hidden from new requests while preserving reporting) or restored.
  • Automated Campaign Codes: Generated automatically in the format CMP-YYYY-NNNN and cannot be manually edited.
  • Mandatory Request Tagging: Every travel, cab, hotel, and reimbursement request must be tagged to an active campaign. An emergency option (*"Other - not yet listed"*) allows typing a campaign title so field work is never blocked.
  • Campaign Financials: Real-time analytics on deployed staff, booked journeys, hotel nights, and total spend (travel, cab, hotel, reimbursements) per campaign.
  • Departments Master: Admins maintain department hierarchies (Operations, Media, Strategy, Logistics, etc.) for cross-departmental cost filtering.

3. Team Management & Organizational Hierarchy

  • Single-Tier Hierarchy: Each ground staff member reports to one manager (*"Reports to"*). Reassignment occurs seamlessly if a manager leaves or changes role.
  • Autonomous Team Control for Managers: Within the Manager Portal (*"My Team"*), managers can directly add new members, edit member details, and remove/delete members from their roster.
  • Field Designations: Standard designations — Executive, Team Lead (TL), and Manager — define operational hierarchy and reporting structure.

4. Corporate Travel Policies & Financial Governance

The system embeds and automatically validates the corporate travel regulations of DesignBoxed Innovations Pvt. Ltd.:

  • Mode & Class Rules:
    • 8 hours or less: Train 3rd AC / AC Bus / Volvo.
    • More than 8 hours or > 500 km: Flight (Economy Class).
    • Flight, train, and bus bookings follow the identical mandatory manager → admin → vendor workflow as hotels and cabs.
  • Lodging Ceilings (Exclusive of Taxes):
City Category Cities Included Single Occupancy Double Occupancy Triple Occupancy
Category A Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad Up to ₹1,500 / night Up to ₹2,000 / night Up to ₹2,500 / night
Category B Amravati, Itanagar, Dispur, Patna, Raipur, Panaji, Gandhinagar, Chandigarh, Shimla, Ranchi, Thiruvananthapuram, Bhopal, Imphal, Shillong, Aizawl, Guwahati, Kohima, Bhubaneswar, Jaipur, Gangtok, Agartala, Lucknow, Dehradun Up to ₹1,000 / night Up to ₹1,500 / night Up to ₹2,000 / night

  • Daily Food Allowance (Employee incurred, claimed via reimbursement): Flat rate of ₹400 / day across all cities. Actual itemized food bills required.
  • Local Public Conveyance: Auto/rickshaw up to ₹150 / day without bills; amounts above ₹150 require receipts. Cabs reimbursed at actuals with receipts. Tolls and parking reimbursed at actuals with receipts.

5. Request Management (Field Employees)

  • Multi-Category Request Form: Long-distance travel (Flight, Train, Bus), Cabs, and Hotels.
  • Group Requests & Co-Travelers: One employee can raise a request for a team by tagging colleagues. Each traveler forms an independent decision row.
  • Edit Window: A request remains freely editable while all travelers are in PENDING status. Once any manager/admin action occurs, the request locks and subsequent changes go through formal cancellation and re-raise. Every edit writes a revision diff.
  • Conflict Detection: Checks proposed dates against existing itineraries (hotel by night, journey by closed window, cab sharing day with flight/train is exempt). Clashing requests warn the user; approvers must enter an audited justification to override.
  • Cancellations & Extensions: Immediate self-cancellation before decisions; formal *"Ask to Cancel"* after approval/booking. Cabs can be extended by an additional day upon administrative approval.

6. Universal Mandatory Two-Tier Approval Pipeline

Every booking request (Flight, Train, Bus, Cab, Hotel) must complete a mandatory two-step approval before being dispatched to vendors:

1
Ground Staff Submits Request
Request is linked to an active campaign with destination, dates, travel reason, and urgency. For hotels, default requested occupancy is Single.
2
Level-1: Manager Approval (Strictly Mandatory)
The employee's manager reviews the request. For hotels, the manager determines Single or Double occupancy eligibility. Manager approves or rejects with comments. (Admin approval is physically locked until manager approves).
3
Level-2: Admin Approval
Admin reviews the manager-approved request, validates policy compliance, confirms/overrides occupancy, and grants final approval with a logged reason.
4
Vendor Dispatch & Fulfilment
Request is routed to the designated Vendor portal to initiate hotel option submission, cab driver assignment, or flight/train ticket booking.

7. Hotel Sourcing, Occupancy & Co-Stay Matching

  • Occupancy Decision: Defaults to Single Occupancy. Evaluated by Manager or Admin based on seniority, campaign budget, or paired travel. Enforces city rate caps.
  • Vendor Hotel Proposals: Vendor submits 2 to 5 available hotel options in the destination city. Details include: Hotel Name, Full Street Address & Landmark, Google Maps link, Room Category, Included Amenities, and Nightly Rate.
  • Employee Selection: The employee receives a notification and selects their preferred hotel from the vendor's proposals.
  • Same-Gender Room Sharing Engine: If Double Occupancy is active, the system queries colleagues staying at that exact hotel during overlapping dates.
    • Strict Safety Filter: Only colleagues of the identical gender (Male with Male, Female with Female) are displayed. Cross-gender pairing is strictly prohibited.
    • Employee selects roommate, opts for an individual room (if eligible), or requests Admin allotment.

8. In-Transit & On-Site Verification Telemetry

8.1 Daily Cab Hire Telemetry (Whole-Day Dedicated Cab)

Cabs are deployed on a whole-day basis where the car remains dedicated to the employee throughout their working hours. Typically, start location and drop location are identical (hotel in morning → field duties → hotel in evening):

A
Morning Start of Day: Meter Photo & Timestamp
When meeting the cab at the hotel, the employee clicks Start Trip, snaps a photo of the odometer, and enters starting KM. The system auto-captures the non-editable IST timestamp and GPS coordinates.
B
Evening End of Day: Meter Photo & Timestamp
Upon returning to the hotel at the conclusion of field work, the employee clicks End Trip, snaps the final odometer photo, and enters ending KM. The system auto-captures the departure timestamp and location.
C
Automated Telemetry Audit
The system computes Total Distance = End KM − Start KM and Duration = End Time − Start Time. Both meter photos and readings are displayed side-by-side in Admin and Finance screens to audit against vendor invoices.

8.2 Manual Hotel Check-In / Check-Out Verification

  • Manual Check-In: Upon arrival at the hotel, the employee clicks Check-In. The system records the arrival timestamp (IST) and device geolocation. The employee enters their assigned room number.
  • Manual Check-Out: On departure, the employee clicks Check-Out, auto-capturing departure time and GPS. The employee uploads a photo of the Final Hotel Folio / Bill (confirming company entity name and employee name).
  • Reimbursement Prompt: Submitting check-out prompts the employee to raise food or local conveyance reimbursement for the completed stay.

9. Post-Travel Expense Reimbursement Module

Company directly books and pays for intercity travel and hotels. Daily food, parking, tolls, and local public transit are incurred directly by the employee and reimbursed under policy caps:

  • Eligible Categories:
    • Daily Food Allowance: Flat rate of ₹400 / day across all cities. Itemized food bills mandatory.
    • Local Transit: Metered auto/rickshaw up to ₹150/day without bills; claims > ₹150 require receipts. Tolls and parking reimbursed at actuals.
    • Miscellaneous Field Expenses: Incidental field work supplies with receipts and written justification.
  • Reimbursement Form Structure:
    • Header Details: Claim ID (EXP-YYYY-NNNN), employee name/code, department, linked campaign, linked travel booking ID, destination city, travel date range.
    • Itemized Line Entries: Date, category, vendor name, invoice number, amount claimed, receipt attachment upload (photo/PDF), employee remarks.
    • Manager Review: Line manager endorses that the employee was on official field duty.
    • Finance Audit & Settlement: Finance team verifies bills against policy limits (flat ₹400/day for food), applies deductions where necessary, enters payment mode and Bank Transaction UTR Number, and marks the claim SETTLED.
    • CSV Import/Export: Full spreadsheet export and bulk import support for audit and accounting reconciliation.

10. Fulfillment, Ticketing & AI Extraction

  • Action Queue: Segmented tabs for Awaiting, Partially Approved, Approved, Ready for Vendor, Booked, Rejected, and Cancelled. Filters for priority and type; search by employee, campaign, or request ID.
  • Vendor Portal Fulfillment: Hotel vendors submit room options and final vouchers; Cab vendors record car and driver details; Ticketing partners upload confirmed e-tickets.
  • Direct Admin Booking & Gemini AI Extraction: For direct desk bookings, admins upload raw tickets/vouchers. Google Gemini AI parses PNR, carrier, flight/train numbers, dates, times, and hotel names. Details are pre-filled for administrative check; the model never auto-books on its own.
  • Ticket Delivery: Booking emails carry the confirmed ticket as an attachment (manager in CC). Employees can download their tickets from My Requests at any time.

11. Vendors & Invoices (Reconciliation)

  • Vendor Directory: Active directory of contracted hotel partners, cab fleets, and travel agencies.
  • Reconciliation Invoices: Admin generates invoice INV-YYYY-NNNN aggregating booked and completed trips for a vendor in a given period. Total is calculated strictly from verified trip costs.
  • Super Admin Approval: Super Admin approves invoice (freezing costs and locking trips from duplicate billing) or rejects with comments. Downloadable as CSV or printable PDF.

12. Employee Management & Profiles

  • Team Directory: Comprehensive employee roster with CSV bulk import and preview. Fields: Name, Email, Mobile (unique 10-digit), Role, Designation, Department, Reports-To Manager, Base Location, Gender, Employee Code, and Status (`ACTIVE`, `DEACTIVATED`, `LEFT`, `DELETED`).
  • Identity Documents & Encryption: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport numbers stored with Fernet AES-256 encryption. Scans stored outside the web root. Viewing sensitive data writes an audited `VIEW_SENSITIVE` row. Automatic 90-day post-exit purge.
  • Travel History: Timeline view per employee showing all historical journeys, hotel stays, and cab companions.

13. Dashboard, Analytics & Reporting

  • Live Operations Dashboard: Visual breakdown of deployed staff by state and campaign, pending queues, and system alerts.
  • Departmental Analytics: Trips, headcount, hotel nights, and booked expenditures by department.
  • Cost Analytics: Expenditures filterable by campaign, location, and date range across Travel, Cabs, Hotels, and Reimbursements.
  • Telemetry Audit Reports: Cross-references cab meter actual KM against vendor billed KM to detect overbilling.
  • Travel Logs: Complete historical log of all movements, filterable and exportable to CSV.

14. Notifications, Reminders & Communications

  • In-App & Email: Real-time notification center plus Gmail SMTP delivery. Decisions, bookings (with ticket attached), room-sharing notices, and pending alerts.
  • Automated Reminders: Travelers notified 2 days before a trip; managers and admins reminded of requests pending over 3 days.
  • Delivery Ledger: Audit log tracking recipient, timestamp, delivery status (`SENT`, `FAILED`, `SUPPRESSED`), and server responses.

15. System Security & Cryptographic Audit Logging

  • Cryptographic Append-Only Activity Log: Every event (creation, edits, manager L1 approvals, admin L2 approvals, vendor hotel submissions, employee hotel picks, manual check-in/outs, cab odometer captures, finance reimbursement audits, disbursements) is recorded in an immutable ledger.
  • Tamper-Evident Hash Chaining: Each row contains the SHA-256 hash of the previous row, verified via the admin console. Append-only database user grants in production (`INSERT` and `SELECT` only).
  • Rate Limiting & Hardening: Per-IP rate limiting on authentication endpoints, secure HTTP headers, and environment-based credential management.
