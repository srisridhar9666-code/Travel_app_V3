Scope of Work (SOW): Field Logistics & Travel Management System

Sriyatra - Your Travel Desk · Version 2.1, updated to match the application as delivered

Version2.1
DateOctober 2026
SupersedesSOW v1 and the SOW Addendum
Prepared bySridhar, DesignBoxed

This version keeps the seven areas of the original SOW and adds what was agreed and built since: a team hierarchy with two-level approval, richer cab handling, approved cancellations, booking in one step with the ticket emailed to the traveller, room sharing allotted by the admin, vendors and invoices, department reporting, and notifications. Items marked NEW were not in v1; CHANGED marks a v1 item whose behaviour was refined. A summary of differences is in section 12.

1. System Overview & Authentication

A web platform for requesting, approving and fulfilling travel, local cabs and hotel stays for field teams (about 100 ground staff), run by a small admin team (about 10), with team managers in between.

Roles and access CHANGED

RoleWhat they can do
Ground staffRaise and track their own travel, cab and hotel requests; tag colleagues; ask to cancel or extend; see their bookings, travel history and notifications. May report to one manager.
Manager NEWEverything ground staff do, plus: see their team's people, trips and travel history (never costs); recommend or not recommend each team request with a comment; approve or reject a team member's ask to cancel; ask an admin to add, edit or remove team members; create and edit campaigns.
AdminRun the travel desk: final decisions on requests, bookings, costs, people (Team), departments, campaigns (including archive and delete), vendors, invoices (create, edit, submit), reports and the activity log.
Super admin NEWEverything an admin sees, plus: the only role that approves or rejects vendor invoices, purges expired identity documents, and manages other super admins. Reads but does not create or edit invoices and vendors, so the person who approves an invoice never prepared it.

Roles are ranked (ground staff < manager < admin < super admin); nobody can grant a role above their own or change the account of someone above them. The former "system admin" role was folded into Admin.

Sign-in and accounts

  • No self-registration: an admin creates each account and sends a single-use invitation link (valid 72 hours) by email or copies it to share on WhatsApp/SMS. The person sets their own password.
  • Passwords of at least 10 characters; "Forgot password" sends a reset link valid 2 hours; 5 wrong attempts lock the account for 15 minutes (an admin can unlock); sessions last 8 hours.
  • Each browser tab can hold its own sign-in, so an admin can test as an employee in another tab.
  • Only accounts with status Active can sign in; Deactivated, Left and Deleted accounts are refused with a reason.

Look and feel

  • Light, Dark or System theme, remembered per person, across every screen including the sidebar.
  • Phones and tablets open the full desktop layout by default (zoomed to fit), with a "Switch to phone view" option remembered per browser. NEW
  • Sriyatra branding throughout, with the DesignBoxed company credit. Times are shown in India time, 12-hour format.

2. Campaign & Project Management

  • Campaign lifecycle: create, edit, pause, complete, archive (hidden from new requests, history kept) and restore; a campaign with no requests can be deleted. Admins have full control; managers can create and edit but not archive or delete. CHANGED
  • Campaign IDs are generated automatically as CMP-YYYY-NNNN and cannot be typed or edited. NEW
  • Every request must be tagged to an active campaign; an "Other - not yet listed" choice takes a typed campaign name so work is never blocked. Every request also needs a reason for travel.
  • Campaign-level financials: personnel involved and total travel, cab and hotel spend per campaign (section 8).
  • Departments are maintained by admins on their own screen and picked from a list on Team and My profile. NEW

3. Request Management (Employees)

Request types and fields

  • Flight, train or bus: mode, from and to (state and city picked from a list of Indian places), departure and return date-time.
  • Local cab CHANGED: pickup and drop address with their city, date-time, cab type (No preference by default, Dzire - 4 seats or Ertiga - 7 seats), and trip: Local (within 80 km) or Outstation with an approximate distance (80 to 5,000 km).
  • Hotel: city, check-in and check-out dates.
  • For all: campaign, reason for travel, priority (High, Medium, Low), co-travellers, notes.

Group requests, editing and conflicts

  • One employee can raise a request for a group by tagging colleagues (for example one cab for four people).
  • Drafts can be saved; a submitted request stays editable until an admin acts on it, and every edit is recorded in its history.
  • Clash detection checks the requester and every tagged colleague against their existing trips and warns while the form is being filled. A clash does not block the request; an admin who approves over a clash must type a reason, which is logged. CHANGED

Room sharing (co-stay)

  • As soon as the requester picks the hotel's state and city, colleagues of the same gender staying in that city are listed with their dates, so the trip can be lined up. Once the dates are entered they choose in the form: share a room with a named colleague, a room of my own, or let the admin decide. Different genders are never offered a shared room. CHANGED
  • On Approvals the admin sees, for each hotel traveller, who they asked to share with or which same-gender colleagues are staying in that city on overlapping nights, and can allot the same room (both stays are paired and both people are told) or give a room of their own. NEW

Cancelling and extending NEW

  • Before any admin decision, the requester cancels at once with a reason.
  • Once anyone on the trip is approved or booked, Cancel becomes an ask to cancel: the admins and the requester's manager are told, and either may approve (the trip is cancelled; admins are reminded to call off the booking) or keep the trip with a comment the requester reads. Admins can still cancel outright.
  • A booked cab can be extended by a day: the traveller gives a reason, an admin approves (the end time moves one day later) or rejects with a comment; it can be asked again for further days.

4. Team Hierarchy & Two-Level Approval NEW

  • Hierarchy (one level): each ground staff member can report to one manager ("Reports to" on Team). A manager who is demoted or switched off releases their team for reassignment.
  • My team (managers): members' details, travel history and trips without costs; asks to add, edit or remove a member go to an admin, who approves or rejects with a comment. Nothing changes until approved, and the manager is told either way.
  • Two-level approval: when a team member raises a request, their manager is notified and gives a Recommend or Do not recommend with a comment on Team approvals. The admin sees the manager's response beside each traveller and makes the final decision with a reason. The admin may decide before the manager responds, so an absent manager never blocks travel.
  • Manager copied: the decision email to the traveller is sent with their manager in CC, and includes the admin's reason and the manager's recommendation.

5. Fulfilment & Booking (Admins)

  • Action queue: tabs for Awaiting, Partly approved, Approved, Booked, Rejected and Cancelled, with live counts (Expired appears only when a request passed its travel date undecided); split by type (All, Flight·Train·Bus, Cab, Hotel); filters for priority and "waiting for a manager"; search by employee name, employee code or email, campaign, request number or place CHANGED; a banner for high-priority work; separate cards for asks to cancel and cab extensions; CSV export of any tab.
  • Per-person decisions: approve or reject the whole group or individual travellers, one by one or in one action. Every decision needs a reason, which the traveller sees. Booking is a separate step after approval.
  • Booking details CHANGED: marking someone booked records the booking reference (PNR) and what the traveller needs on the day: airline or operator, flight/train/bus number, departure and arrival, seat or berth, or the hotel's name and address, plus notes. For cabs: the cab actually sent (Dzire or Ertiga), vehicle number, driver's name and mobile.
  • Booking in one step CHANGED: Mark booked opens a booking section in the traveller's own row (no pop-up). The admin uploads the ticket or voucher there (PDF or image); Google Gemini (Vertex AI) reads the PNR, carrier, number, times and hotel and fills the booking details in automatically, and flags a ticket that does not match the request (wrong date, route or city). The admin checks the details and marks the traveller booked; the ticket is confirmed with the booking. Extraction never books or notifies on its own.
  • Costs: recorded per traveller in INR (a shared booking can be split evenly), together with the vendor paid. A worklist shows booked trips that still have no cost.
  • Notifications: the traveller is told of every decision in the app and by email; the booking email carries all the booking details with the ticket attached (manager in CC). CHANGED
  • Tickets afterwards: the traveller (or whoever raised the trip) downloads the confirmed ticket from My requests at any time; admins reopen it with View ticket on the Booked tab, and every ticket uploaded for a trip stays listed with it. NEW

6. Vendors & Invoices NEW

  • Vendors: travel agents, cab operators, hotels and others, with contact person, phone, email and GSTIN; switched off rather than deleted.
  • Invoices for vendor reconciliation: an admin picks a vendor and a date range; the screen lists every booked trip with a cost paid to that vendor (or with no vendor recorded yet), travelling in the period and not already invoiced, and explains why when nothing is listed. The admin ticks the trips to include.
  • Invoice numbers are generated as INV-YYYY-NNNN; the vendor's own bill number and notes can be added. The total is always calculated from the trips' recorded costs and cannot be typed.
  • An invoice stays editable until approved. It is submitted to the super admin, who approves it or rejects it with a comment (it can then be corrected and resubmitted). Once approved its amounts are frozen, the costs of its trips are locked, and a trip's cost can be billed on only one invoice.
  • Admins and the super admin can download each invoice as CSV or as a printable PDF. Every create, edit, submission, decision and download is in the activity log.

7. Employee Management & Profiles

  • Team directory: invite, edit and import people (CSV with preview and error checks). Fields: name, work email, app access (role), designation (Executive, Team lead, Manager - a job title only), department, reports-to manager, base state and city, gender (Male/Female, which decides room sharing), employee code and mobile.
  • Mobile numbers CHANGED: 10 digits starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9, stored without a country code, and unique across employees.
  • Status: Active, Deactivated, Left (with exit date) or Deleted (restorable); only Active can sign in. Admins see a person's upcoming trips before switching them off.
  • My profile: everyone corrects their own name and mobile and changes their password and email (with their password); admins can also set their own department, gender and base.
  • Identity documents: Aadhaar, PAN, passport and others are stored with the number encrypted; every time a number is revealed or a scan downloaded it is logged; documents of people who have left are purged 90 days after their exit date.
  • Travel history: a timeline per person of every trip, mode, companions and hotel stay, for any period (default the last 90 days). Employees see their own; managers their team's (without costs); admins everyone's with costs.

8. Dashboard & Reporting

  • Live operations dashboard: staff currently deployed by state and campaign, the queue waiting for decisions, recent and upcoming trips, and alerts (for example when email is not set up), with filters for dates, campaign, department, person and destination.
  • By department NEW: trips, people, hotel nights, pending decisions and booked spend for every department (people with none grouped as "No department"); clicking a department filters the whole dashboard, and choosing one narrows the employee list to its people.
  • Cost analytics: spend by campaign, location and period, split by travel, cab and hotel, with the uncosted worklist; the same department filter applies.
  • Travel logs: every movement, filterable by department, searchable and exportable to CSV.

9. Notifications & Reminders NEW

  • In-app notification bell for everyone, plus email through the company Gmail account (an app password, not the account password).
  • Who is told: travellers of every decision and booking (manager in CC; the booking email carries the ticket as an attachment); colleagues paired in a shared room; admins of new requests, manager recommendations, asks to cancel, cab extensions and team changes; managers of their team's requests; the super admin of invoices to approve.
  • People can switch off optional categories (bookings, room sharing, reminders, new requests); decisions are always sent.
  • Automatic reminders: travellers two days before a trip; admins about requests waiting more than three days; failed emails are retried.
  • A delivery ledger shows who was told what and whether the email went, with a test-email button. While testing, an allow-list can restrict email to chosen addresses.

10. System Security & Audit Logging

  • Activity log: every action by every user is recorded - who raised, edited, recommended, approved, rejected (including partial), booked, cancelled, uploaded or viewed an identity document, and every change to people, campaigns, vendors and invoices - with time, reason and before/after values.
  • Sign-ins, sign-outs and failed sign-ins are recorded too, for security, but are left out of the Activity log page, its counts and its CSV, so the page shows what people did. CHANGED
  • Tamper-evident: each entry is chained to the previous one by a cryptographic hash, and the chain can be verified from the screen; in production the database account is made append-only so entries cannot be edited or deleted. The log is open to admins and the super admin and can be exported to CSV.
  • Every server action checks the user's role and organisation; sign-in and password-reset attempts are rate-limited; secrets live in the server's environment file, never in the code.

11. Platform & Delivery

  • Web application: React front end, Python FastAPI back end, MySQL 8 database.
  • Works in current browsers on desktop, Android and iPhone/iPad.
  • Packaged with Docker (API and web server), configured from one settings file (backend/.env); ready for Google Cloud Run with Cloud Storage buckets for tickets and identity documents.
  • Database changes ship as migrations that can safely be re-run after an interruption; the app warns admins if the database or server is out of date.
  • Quality: over 1,000 automated tests plus a 127-check end-to-end run that builds the system from an empty database.
  • Built ready for more than one company (organisation) without database changes.

Not in this scope (possible next steps)

  • WhatsApp or SMS alerts (the notification system is ready for another channel; WhatsApp Business messages are charged per message by Meta).
  • Travel policy limits, budgets per campaign or department, approving from email, calendar files for trips, native mobile apps, and payment or booking-site integrations.
